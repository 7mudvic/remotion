## 2. The Five Concepts

| # | Concept | Symbol | Wordmark style | Best for |
|---|---------|--------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | **Atom Orbit** | three orbiting paths around a luminous core | Inter Bold | the flagship — direct AI/intelligence metaphor |
| 2 | **Prism** | nested triangles + light tracks | Inter Medium | "noise → signal" — premium, sharp |
| 3 | **Pulse Wave** | heartbeat with anomaly spike | Inter Light | data-native, technical, alive |
| 4 | **Aperture** | concentric rings + crosshair ticks | JetBrains Mono | precision, focus, the trader's eye |
| 5 | **Helix A** | two intertwined arcs forming an "A" | Playfair italic | editorial weight, dual-mind ensemble |

### Rationale

#### 1. Atom Orbit
Three orbiting paths around a luminous core. Each orbit reads as one model in the ensemble; together they converge on a single answer. **Most direct AI metaphor — recommended primary.**

#### 2. Prism
A prism refracts noise into clarity. Two nested triangles plus light tracks evoke the moment market chaos becomes a clean signal. Premium, geometric, minimal.

#### 3. Pulse Wave
A market heartbeat with one tall spike — the anomaly Alfex catches before the rest of the room. Confident, technical, alive.

#### 4. Aperture
Concentric rings with a focused core and four crosshair ticks. Reads as precision, focus, and a lens trained on the market.

#### 5. Helix A
Two intertwined arcs forming a stylised "A" — Alfex itself, and the dual-mind ensemble that makes it work. The italic serif wordmark adds editorial weight.

### Treatments

Every layout is delivered in three colour treatments:

1. **`full-color.svg`** — gradient mark on dark surface. The flagship.
2. **`white-mono.svg`** — pure white on dark. Use when you need to lock to one colour (UI overlays, video).
3. **`black-mono.svg`** — pure black on white. Use on light backgrounds, print, faxes, anywhere mono is required.

## 6. Usage Guidelines

### Clear space

Maintain a clear-space margin equal to the **height of the symbol's core** (≈ 20% of the mark's full height) on every side. Never crowd the logo with text or graphics inside that buffer.

### Minimum sizes

| Layout | Digital min | Print min |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| `horizontal` | 120px wide | 25 mm wide |
| `vertical` | 90px wide | 20 mm wide |
| `square` | 64px | 15 mm |
| `icon-only` | 24px (use simplified version below 32px) | 8 mm |
| `wordmark-only` | 100px wide | 22 mm wide |

### On dark backgrounds

Use `full-color.svg` first. If the background is busy or coloured, switch to `white-mono.svg` for maximum legibility.

### On light backgrounds

Use `black-mono.svg`, or place the `full-color.svg` over a flat dark card / panel.

### Don't

- ❌ Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark.
- ❌ Recolour individual elements outside the approved palette.
- ❌ Add drop shadows, glows, or filter effects (the gradient is the only depth).
- ❌ Crop the orbital arcs / triangles / waves.
- ❌ Re-render the wordmark in a different typeface.
- ❌ Place the logo on a near-equal-luminance background (e.g. green on green).

### Do

- ✅ Use `icon-only` below 100px contexts (favicon, app icon).
- ✅ Use `wordmark-only` in editorial / long-form contexts.
- ✅ Keep at least one full clear-space buffer on every edge.
- ✅ Use the white-mono version inside coloured callouts.

## 7. Typography

### Wordmark fonts (per concept)

| Concept | Font | Weight | Style |
|---------|------|--------|-------|
| Atom Orbit | Inter | 800 | Default |
| Prism | Inter | 500 | Default |
| Pulse Wave | Inter | 300 | Tracked +2% |
| Aperture | JetBrains Mono | 600 | Tracked +6%, all-caps |
| Helix A | Playfair Display | 500 | Italic |

> If your installed system lacks any of the above, fall back to: **Inter** → Helvetica Neue → Arial; **Playfair Display** → Georgia; **JetBrains Mono** → SF Mono / Consolas.

For brand-tight contexts (printed materials, products) **convert text to outlines/paths** in your design tool to guarantee identical rendering across machines.

  --export-png=alfex-horizontal.png \

## 10. Regenerating the System

If the brand evolves (new colour, new wordmark style, etc.), edit the `CONCEPTS` and `COLORS` objects in `generate.js`, then:

```bash
node generate.js
```

That single command regenerates all 75 files from source — no need to hand-edit individual SVGs.

## 11. Recommended Primary

Out of the five concepts, **`Atom Orbit`** is the recommended primary lockup:

- It is the most direct, legible AI metaphor.
- It scales cleanly from 16 px favicon to billboard.
- It already aligns with the existing in-app atom mark (continuity).
- The gradient core gives the brand a single, ownable "signature shape."

The other four concepts are alternates suitable for sub-brands, campaigns, internal tooling, or seasonal variants.
